## Recorded learner recommendation

> I recommend that Northstar Talent UK should not deploy HireSort AI into production at this stage. There is insufficient evidence regarding fairness, data protection, model performance and meaningful human oversight. Deployment should only be reconsidered following completion and satisfactory review of these assessments and the establishment of accountable monitoring procedures.

## Basis for the recommendation

- **Decision:** HOLD / NO PRODUCTION DEPLOYMENT.
- **Reason:** Unverified fairness, privacy, meaningful human review and vendor assurances.
- **Release conditions:** Accepted DPIA (where required), satisfactory fairness and performance evidence, accountable owner, safeguards and monitoring.
- **Decision authority:** Designated governance committee / authorised business risk owner; the analyst recommends but does not unilaterally approve.
- **Reassessment triggers:** Material model changes, incidents, data shifts, new use cases or scheduled reviews.

**Assurance note:** 'Requested' and 'Missing' reflect the fictional case exercise. They are not claims that any real provider failed a due-diligence process.
