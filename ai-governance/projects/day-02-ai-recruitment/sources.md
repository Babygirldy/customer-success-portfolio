## Research checks

- Verify the consolidated law and *date of commencement* for the relevant automated decision-making requirements instead of relying on the workbook's undemonstrated dates.
- Check application to **profiling and ranking** where a human makes the final decision, rather than assuming every AI-assisted ranking is exclusively automated.
- Consider the Equality Act 2010 and accessibility implications.
- Record the applicable jurisdiction and assessment date for each legal claim.
