## Interview question

**'Your company wants to deploy an AI CV-screening tool. What steps would you take before approval?'**

### Learner's submitted written answer (verbatim)

> If my organisation proposed deploying an AI-powered CV-screening system, I would begin by understanding its purpose, functionality, data inputs and the individuals potentially affected.
>
> Using the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, I would identify and assess risks relating to algorithmic bias, discrimination, data protection, accuracy, transparency and accountability.
>
> I would work with relevant stakeholders to assess UK GDPR and Equality Act obligations, determine the need for a Data Protection Impact Assessment, and verify the supplier's testing evidence.
>
> I would also ensure meaningful human oversight, appropriate security controls and mechanisms for candidates to challenge relevant decisions.
>
> Finally, I would document my findings in an AI risk register and present an evidence-based recommendation to management.
>
> Where significant risks remain unresolved, I would recommend delaying deployment until appropriate safeguards have been demonstrated.
