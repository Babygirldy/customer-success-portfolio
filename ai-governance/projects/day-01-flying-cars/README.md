# Day 01 — Flying Cars: Regulatory Fragmentation and AI Governance

**Scenario:** A fictional UK business, SkyBridge Mobility Ltd, proposes a road-capable aircraft with AI-assisted navigation.  
**Problem:** Which regulatory requirements apply across road use, aircraft operations, AI-enabled functions and personal-data processing?  
**Status:** AI-assisted learning draft; not a completed legal opinion or certification assessment.

## Key analytical findings

- A vehicle's marketing label does not determine its regulatory classification.
- Road and aviation permissions must be established separately.
- Certification of the aircraft design is not the same as pilot competence or operating permission.
- AI assurance complements, rather than replaces, aviation safety engineering.
- The governing question is **what can be shown by evidence**, not what a supplier merely claims.
