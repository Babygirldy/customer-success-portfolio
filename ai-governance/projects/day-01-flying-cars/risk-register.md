# Day 01 — Illustrative risk register

**Scoping note:** Example risks for a fictional hybrid road/aviation vehicle. Likelihood and residual severity cannot responsibly be quantified without technical data.

| ID | Risk | Potential harm | Example controls | Evidence to request | Owner (proposed) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F-01 | Misclassification of vehicle and planned operations | Unlawful or unsafe use | Formal legal/technical classification review | Vehicle description, applicable certification basis, advice and approvals | Legal and Certification |
| F-02 | Failure at road-to-flight transition | Injury, loss of control, damage | Physical interlocks, tested procedures, engineered safety barriers | Transition hazard analysis, verification and test reports | Systems Safety |
| F-03 | Incorrect AI navigation or obstacle output | Collision or unsafe manoeuvre | Representative validation, independent safety protections and operating limits | Model evaluation, safety assessment, flight-test evidence | AI and Flight Safety |
| F-04 | Navigation or update cybersecurity compromise | Loss of integrity or control | Secure software supply chain, authenticated updates and system segmentation | Threat model, security testing, incident plan | Cybersecurity |
| F-05 | Unclear liability and insurance coverage | Uncompensated harm or disputes | Legal responsibility mapping and tailored insurance review | Contracts, policy wordings, operator arrangements | Legal and Insurance |
| F-06 | Inadequate personal-data governance | Unlawful processing or privacy harm | Lawful-basis assessment, privacy notices, DPIA as needed, minimisation | Data-flow map, supplier terms, DPIA | Privacy / DPO |

**Analyst caveat:** A planned mitigation is not an implemented or tested control. Each risk remains **open for evidence-based assessment**.
